"How much does argan oil cost wholesale?" is usually the first question in a buyer's email. It's a fair question, but a single number rarely helps: two quotes for "argan oil" can be for different products, in different packaging, delivered to different places.

This guide explains what actually drives the price per liter, how to compare quotes fairly, and how to keep costs down without ending up with oil you can't sell.

## Why Argan Oil Is Expensive to Produce

Understanding the cost structure helps you judge whether a quote is realistic.

- **Limited origin.** The argan tree grows almost only in southwestern Morocco.
- **Low yield.** Tens of kilograms of fruit are needed for one liter of oil.
- **Labor.** Drying the fruit, removing the pulp and cracking the very hard nuts is slow work. Much of it is still done by hand, often by women's cooperatives.
- **Weather.** The trees depend on rainfall; dry years mean smaller harvests.

When a quote is far below what established producers ask, one of these costs is being skipped - usually quality.

## The 8 Factors That Decide the Price per Liter

### 1. Grade: cosmetic, deodorized or culinary

- **Cosmetic virgin oil** (unroasted kernels) is the baseline for skincare.
- **Deodorized cosmetic oil** requires an extra processing step, which adds cost.
- **Culinary oil** (roasted kernels) is a different product for a different market, with its own price.

Always make sure the quotes you compare are for the same grade.

### 2. Quality level

Within cosmetic oil, fresher oil with lower acidity (extra-virgin) is worth more than oil that barely meets the standard. Ask for the batch COA with every quote.

### 3. Organic certification

Certified organic oil costs more: certification fees, audits, segregated storage, traceability paperwork. If you won't claim "organic" on your label, conventional oil with a clean COA may be the smarter buy.

### 4. Harvest and kernel supply

Argan fruit ripens in summer, and the size of the harvest depends on rainfall. After dry years, kernel prices rise and oil prices follow. Recent drought years in Morocco have made this factor more visible. Good suppliers warn you before the season, not after you've committed.

### 5. Volume

Price per liter drops as volume grows. The biggest steps are usually:

- from small bottles to 5L jerrycans,
- from single jerrycans to 25L units,
- from 25L units to 200L drums or regular contracts.

### 6. Packaging

| Packaging | Effect on price per liter |
|---|---|
| 200L drum | Lowest |
| 25L jerrycan | Low |
| 5L jerrycan | Medium |
| 1L bottle | Higher |
| Retail bottles (30-100 ml) with dropper or pump | Highest - you're paying for glass, closure, labeling and filling |

### 7. Incoterm and shipping

An EXW price (you collect from the facility) and a DAP price (delivered to your door) are not comparable. Shipping, insurance, duties and fees can change the landed cost significantly, especially for small orders.

### 8. Payment terms and exchange rates

Quotes in euros or dollars depend on the exchange rate with the Moroccan dirham. Longer payment terms or letters of credit may also change the price.

## How to Get a Better Price Without Losing Quality

- **Commit to regular volumes.** A yearly plan with scheduled shipments is worth more to a producer than one large, unpredictable order.
- **Order before the season.** Plan your main purchases before harvest-related price movements.
- **Choose the right packaging.** If you repack locally, buying in drums can cut your cost per liter.
- **Consolidate shipments.** One larger shipment usually costs less per liter than several small ones.
- **Don't over-specify.** If you don't need organic certification or deodorized oil, don't pay for it.
- **Walk away from offers that look too cheap.** The savings disappear with the first batch you can't sell.

## Red Flags in a Price Quote

- A price far below the market for the same grade and certification.
- No COA, or a generic COA with no batch number.
- Vague grade descriptions such as "premium argan oil" without specifying cosmetic or culinary.
- Prices without an Incoterm.
- Pressure to pay 100% upfront on a large first order.

## FAQ

### Why do argan oil prices change from year to year?

Mainly because of the harvest. The trees depend on rainfall, so dry years reduce the supply of kernels and push prices up.

### Is organic argan oil worth the extra cost?

Only if you will sell your product as organic and meet the organic rules of your market. Otherwise, conventional oil with a clean COA is often the better choice.

### Is it cheaper to buy in drums and bottle locally?

Often yes for the oil itself, but add your local filling, bottles, labels and labor before deciding. For small volumes, ready-filled private label bottles can work out cheaper.

### Why can't you give a price on your website?

Because the price depends on grade, certification, packaging, quantity, destination and the current harvest. A quote based on your exact needs is more accurate than a list price.
